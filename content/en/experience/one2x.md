I joined ONE2X in September 2026 as a Product Manager for Medeo.

[ONE2X](https://www.one2x.ai/) is an AI product studio behind Medeo, an AI video creation and editing tool, and Mebox, an AI video clipper.

[Medeo](https://www.medeo.app/) lets users create and edit videos through natural-language conversations, turning ideas, text, and assets into video content. A visual track provides control over audio, subtitles, and assets for use cases such as marketing ads, educational explainers, and animated shorts.
